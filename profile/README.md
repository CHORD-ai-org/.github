# CHORD

### Building the foundations of intelligent systems.

CHORD is an independent research and engineering initiative exploring the foundations of intelligent systems.

We work across agent frameworks, cognitive architectures, persistent context, and the infrastructure that enables humans and AI systems to work together over time.

Our goal is not simply to build more capable agents, but to rethink how intelligent systems are structured, coordinated, and evolved.

---

## What We Explore

**Agent Frameworks**

Building reusable foundations for intelligent agents, including planning, execution, coordination, and self-modeling.

**Cognitive Architecture**

Exploring how systems represent, organize, monitor, and evolve their internal states and knowledge.

**Human–Agent Collaboration**

Developing frameworks and environments for persistent, long-term collaboration between humans and AI systems.

**Computational Infrastructure**

Designing the underlying mechanisms and abstractions that make complex intelligent systems practical and extensible.

## Our Approach

We believe good systems begin with good abstractions.

Our engineering principles:

- **Modularity:** Components should have clear responsibilities and composable interfaces.
- **Decoupling:** Independent systems should not inherit unnecessary complexity from one another.
- **Explicitness:** Important state, context, and system behavior should be inspectable.
- **Extensibility:** Architecture should support future capabilities without requiring fundamental redesign.
- **Practicality:** Research should ultimately connect to usable implementations.

We favor clear mechanisms over unnecessary abstraction and working systems over speculative complexity.
